Student Tech Setup & Semester Tool Workflow

Part 1 — Initial student tech setup (Week 1)

Students complete this checklist in Week 1 to establish their workspaces.

  1. Google AI student offer — sign up at Google's student offer using your personal email, not your FIT address. This is the free student access to Google AI.
  2. Gemini Notebook — go to notebook.google.com. Create one notebook titled CT302 - [Student Name] - [Brand Name] Redesign. This is your research repository.
  3. Google Stitch — go to stitch.withgoogle.com and log in.
  4. Google AI Studio / Gemini — go to aistudio.google.com for the developer playground, used for testing Nano Banana image generation and logging multi-turn system instructions.

Part 2 — Tool workflow across the semester

Remapped to the Fall 2026 schedule.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–5 — Research & Proposal

WeeksToolsHow they're used
1Google AI (student offer), Gemini Notebook, Stitch, AI StudioAccount setup and the research notebook.
2Gemini Gems, Gemini NotebookGem creation and Notebook setup. Screenshot the brand's current site and upload to Gemini for visual audits — layout structure, density, accessibility flaws. Dump competitor screenshots, critiques and brand history into Gemini Notebook so research is grounded rather than fabricated.
4Gemini NotebookGenerate the project brief and value proposition from validated research only. Export as markdown.
5—Project Proposal Presentation (40 pts).

Phase 2 · Weeks 6–15 — Design & Presentation

WeeksToolsHow they're used
6–7Google StitchType structural briefs from Gemini Notebook into Stitch to generate early UI concepts. Hand-drawn sketches can be uploaded in Experimental Mode to render a responsive mockup.
8Stitch → FigmaImport the Stitch-generated design into Figma. Figma Basics, Part 1.
9–11Nano Banana, FigmaGenerate original UI icons, imagery and illustration assets with Nano Banana in AI Studio. Import tokens, layouts and assets into Figma for components, styles and Auto Layout.
12–14Gemini, FigmaExport Figma screens as images and ask Gemini to run a WCAG colour-contrast check and tap-target review. Document flagged errors, fix in Figma, log the before/after.
15—Final presentation.

Part 3 — The student's deliverable: the "cognitive trail"

Students hand in more than a Figma link:

  1. The Figma prototype — 4 completed screens.
  2. The Gemini Notebook source ledger — proving the research basis.
  3. The AI Studio / Gemini prompt history link — proving prompt architecture and critical curation.
