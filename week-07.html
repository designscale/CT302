Week 07 · Thursday, October 15 · Phase 1 — Research & Proposal

Project Proposal Presentations

A 3–4 minute professional pitch covering research, brand rationale, and proposed direction — presented in class and submitted as a Loom recording.

Phase one closes. You have three to four minutes to make the case that this brand, this problem and this direction are worth the next seven weeks.

Milestone deliverable

Project Proposal — 40% of final grade

  • Brand profile · UX research synthesis · comparative landscape
  • Live site audit
  • Project brief with value proposition and feature scope
  • Sitemap and low-fi wireframes
  • 3–4 min pitch, presented live and submitted as Loom
  • Evidence pack: prompt logs, workflow map, revision trails, reflection notes

Session plan

01Run of show
Order, timing, and how the room gives feedback.
02Presentations
3–4 minutes each, plus two minutes of questions. Time is called.
03Debrief
Patterns across the pitches — what landed, what got asked repeatedly.
04Phase two preview
What changes next week when we move into Figma.

In-class activity: The pitch

  1. Structure: brand rationale → what the audit found → who it's for → the problem → the proposed direction → what you'll build.
  2. Lead with the finding that made you sure, not with the brand's history.
  3. Say out loud where AI accelerated the work and where you overrode it — this is scored.
  4. Record the same pitch as a Loom and submit both.

Working with AI this week

Rehearse against it. Ask for the three hardest questions your pitch invites, then prepare answers.

Prompts to start from

  • “Here's my pitch script. What claim would a critic attack first?”
  • “Where does this script tell instead of show?”

What to verify

  • Don't let AI write the narration. Graders can hear it, and Reflective Reasoning is scored on how you explain your own choices.

Evidence to log

These feed the cognitive-process rubric. Capture them as you work.

Reflective ReasoningWhere you name AI's role and your overrides, live
Orchestration LogicThe workflow map submitted with the proposal
