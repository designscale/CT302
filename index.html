CT302 Digital Product Design I (Pilot)

Course Description

CT302 Digital Product Design I equips students with the skills to design a responsive e-commerce experience the way modern design teams actually work today: in close collaboration with AI. The course is built around a semester-long studio project — the redesign of an e-commerce brand's homepage and product detail page — carried out in two connected phases.

In the first phase, students use AI as a research and planning collaborator to select a brand candidate, audit its live website, synthesize UX research, and define a brand profile before writing a project proposal. In the second phase, students move into hands-on UI design in Figma — briefly using AI to explore concept directions before committing to an art direction — then build an essential design system and a fully responsive homepage and product detail page. Throughout, students learn not just to use AI tools, but to direct them: verifying research, challenging AI-generated directions, and applying human design judgment at every decision point.

Studio Project

Responsive E-Commerce Website Redesign. Each student selects one e-commerce brand as a redesign candidate and delivers a complete design case for that brand: the research and project proposal that justifies the redesign, plus a fully responsive design for the brand's two most important pages — the Homepage, which establishes art direction and first brand impression, and the Product Detail Page, the most functional page in e-commerce and the one most directly responsible for conversion.

Student Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Conduct AI-assisted market and competitive research to identify, compare, and select a brand candidate for redesign.
  2. Perform a live UX/UI audit of an existing e-commerce site and synthesize research into clear pain points and design opportunities.
  3. Define a brand profile — target audience, mission, archetype — and translate it into a project brief and value proposition.
  4. Use AI selectively to explore concept directions, then critically evaluate and refine that output through hands-on design work in Figma.
  5. Apply typography, color, layout, and responsive principles to build a mobile-first, essential design system.
  6. Design a responsive homepage and PDP across desktop and mobile using Figma components, variables, styles, and Auto Layout.
  7. Present a complete design process professionally — articulating where AI accelerated the work and where your judgment shaped the outcome.
