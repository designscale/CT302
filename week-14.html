Week 14 · Thursday, December 10 · Phase 2 — Design & Presentation

Design Checkpoint & Refinement

Peer critique and revisions across all four final screens ahead of the final presentation.

Last week to change anything. Structured critique, then focused revision — and the evidence pack gets assembled, not scrambled together on Sunday.

Milestone deliverable

Design — 30% of final grade

  • Art direction & moodboard
  • Essential design system
  • Homepage: desktop + mobile
  • PDP: desktop + mobile
  • Evidence pack: prompt logs, workflow maps, revision trails, audit reports, reflection journal, source files

Session plan

01Structured peer critique
Four screens each. Reviewers work from a checklist: system consistency, hierarchy, responsive integrity, accessibility, direction fidelity.
02Revision working session
Fix the highest-severity findings first. Log every change.
03Accessibility pass
Contrast, target sizes, focus order, alt-text intent, text that isn't an image.
04Evidence pack assembly
Prompt logs, workflow map, revision trails, audit reports, reflection journal, original source files — organized and submitted together.

In-class activity: Critique, revise, document

  1. Receive critique without defending. Write it down, sort by severity, then decide.
  2. Revise. Keep a change log — what you changed, why, and whose note prompted it.
  3. Run the full accessibility pass across all four screens.
  4. Assemble the evidence pack. Everything the rubric names should be findable in under a minute.

Working with AI this week

Good closing use: an adversarial reviewer. Ask it to argue against your own work.

Prompts to start from

  • “Here's my design rationale. Argue that these screens don't deliver on the stated art direction.”
  • “What would an accessibility auditor flag first?”

What to verify

  • Fix what's real; document why you dismissed the rest. A dismissed critique with a reason scores; an ignored one doesn't.

Evidence to log

These feed the cognitive-process rubric. Capture them as you work.

Critical CurationRevision trail with severity sorting
Reflective ReasoningChange log explaining the why behind each revision

Before next class

  • Submit all four screens and the evidence pack. Build the final presentation.
