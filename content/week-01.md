## Weekly assignment: Week 1

1. **Brand selection and rationale**
  - Why did you select this brand?
  - What opportunities for improvement have you identified?
  - Conduct a SWOT analysis.
2. **Desk research: brand profile** — include the following:
  - Target audience
  - Brand archetype
  - Competitive landscape
  - Mission and vision
  - Competitor analysis: analyze at least three competitors within the same category. Identify what each competitor does well and where there may be opportunities for improvement.
3. **Live website audit** — audit the brand's current website, focusing only on:
  - Homepage
  - Product Detail Page (PDP)
4. **User interviews**
  - Conduct user interviews to identify users' pain points, needs, wants, and goals within the brand's category.
