Last week to change anything. Structured critique, then focused revision — and the evidence pack gets assembled, not scrambled together on Sunday.

## Milestone deliverable

### Design — 30% of final grade

- Art direction & moodboard
- Essential design system
- Homepage: desktop + mobile
- PDP: desktop + mobile
- Evidence pack: prompt logs, workflow maps, revision trails, audit reports, reflection journal, source files

## Session plan

01. **Structured peer critique** — Four screens each. Reviewers work from a checklist: system consistency, hierarchy, responsive integrity, accessibility, direction fidelity.
02. **Revision working session** — Fix the highest-severity findings first. Log every change.
03. **Accessibility pass** — Contrast, target sizes, focus order, alt-text intent, text that isn't an image.
04. **Evidence pack assembly** — Prompt logs, workflow map, revision trails, audit reports, reflection journal, original source files — organized and submitted together.

## In-class activity: Critique, revise, document

1. Receive critique without defending. Write it down, sort by severity, then decide.
2. Revise. Keep a change log — what you changed, why, and whose note prompted it.
3. Run the full accessibility pass across all four screens.
4. Assemble the evidence pack. Everything the rubric names should be findable in under a minute.

## Working with AI this week

- “Here's my design rationale. Argue that these screens don't deliver on the stated art direction.”
- “What would an accessibility auditor flag first?”
- Fix what's real; document why you dismissed the rest. A dismissed critique with a reason scores; an ignored one doesn't.

## Evidence to log

**Critical Curation**. Revision trail with severity sorting
**Reflective Reasoning**. Change log explaining the why behind each revision
