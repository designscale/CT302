The most functional page in e-commerce, and the one your audit had the most to say about. Everything here serves one decision: buy or don't.

## Due this week

### PDP: desktop + mobile

- Both breakpoints
- Variant selection handled for a real product
- Buy box with all necessary states
- Audit findings addressed

## Session plan

01. **Anatomy of a PDP** — Gallery, product identity, price and variants, buy box, specs, shipping and returns, reviews, cross-sell. What each one has to accomplish.
02. **Build — mobile** — The buy box is the hardest thing on this page at 375px. Start there.
03. **Build — desktop** — Two-column relationship between gallery and buy box; where specs and reviews go.
04. **Crit against the audit** — Pull up your Week 3 findings. Which are fixed? Which did you drop, and why?

## In-class activity: PDP, both breakpoints

1. Design variant selection for a real product with real options — the messy case, not the clean one.
2. Make price, shipping and returns unambiguous. Most audits find this broken; don't repeat it.
3. Give add-to-cart a visible state change. Silent feedback is a pattern failure.
4. Check every fix against the audit finding it was supposed to answer.

## Working with AI this week

- “What states does a PDP buy box need — out of stock, backorder, low stock, variant unavailable, quantity limits?”
- “What accessibility problems are common in product galleries and variant pickers?”
- Confirm accessibility claims against WCAG itself before citing them in your audit documentation.
