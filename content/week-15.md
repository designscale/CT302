The whole semester in five minutes. Half the score is delivery and clarity; the other half is reflective reasoning — how well you explain why, live, without notes to hide behind.

## Milestone deliverable

### Final Presentation — 20% of final grade

- 4–5 minute live presentation
- Loom recording submitted
- Delivery & clarity — 10 pts
- Reflective reasoning, demonstrated live — 10 pts

## Session plan

01. **Run of show** — Order and timing. Files open and ready before we start.
02. **Final presentations** — 4–5 minutes each plus questions.
03. **Close** — What this cohort learned about directing AI, and what to carry into the next studio.

## In-class activity: The final walkthrough

1. Structure: brand rationale → research insights → design decisions → final four screens.
2. Name at least three moments where you overrode AI output, and what you knew that it didn't.
3. Show before/after: the audited page beside your redesign.
4. Submit the Loom recording alongside the live presentation.

## Working with AI this week

- “Give me the five questions most likely to follow this presentation.”
- Reflective Reasoning is scored on what you say, not what you submitted in writing. Rehearse the why out loud.
