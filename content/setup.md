# Student Tech Setup & Semester Tool Workflow

## Part 1 — Initial student tech setup (Week 1)

Students complete this checklist in Week 1 to establish their workspaces.

1. **Google AI student offer** — sign up at [Google's student offer](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/student-offer-google-ai/) using your **personal email**, not your FIT address. This is the free student access to Google AI.
2. **Gemini Notebook** — go to [notebook.google.com](https://notebook.google.com). Create one notebook titled `CT302 - [Student Name] - [Brand Name] Redesign`. This is your research repository.
3. **Google Stitch** — go to [stitch.withgoogle.com](https://stitch.withgoogle.com) and log in.
4. **Google AI Studio / Gemini** — go to [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com) for the developer playground, used for testing Nano Banana image generation and logging multi-turn system instructions.

## Part 2 — Tool workflow across the semester

Remapped to the Fall 2026 schedule.

### Phase 1 · Weeks 1–5 — Research & Proposal

| Weeks | Tools | How they're used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google AI (student offer), Gemini Notebook, Stitch, AI Studio | Account setup and the research notebook. |
| 2 | Gemini Gems, Gemini Notebook | Gem creation and Notebook setup. Screenshot the brand's current site and upload to Gemini for visual audits — layout structure, density, accessibility flaws. Dump competitor screenshots, critiques and brand history into Gemini Notebook so research is grounded rather than fabricated. |
| 4 | Gemini Notebook | Generate the project brief and value proposition from validated research only. Export as markdown. |
| 5 | — | Project Proposal Presentation (40 pts). |

### Phase 2 · Weeks 6–15 — Design & Presentation

| Weeks | Tools | How they're used |
|---|---|---|
| 6–7 | Google Stitch | Type structural briefs from Gemini Notebook into Stitch to generate early UI concepts. Hand-drawn sketches can be uploaded in Experimental Mode to render a responsive mockup. |
| 8 | Stitch → Figma | Import the Stitch-generated design into Figma. Figma Basics, Part 1. |
| 9–11 | Nano Banana, Figma | Generate original UI icons, imagery and illustration assets with Nano Banana in AI Studio. Import tokens, layouts and assets into Figma for components, styles and Auto Layout. |
| 12–14 | Gemini, Figma | Export Figma screens as images and ask Gemini to run a WCAG colour-contrast check and tap-target review. Document flagged errors, fix in Figma, log the before/after. |
| 15 | — | Final presentation. |

## Part 3 — The student's deliverable: the "cognitive trail"

Students hand in more than a Figma link:

1. **The Figma prototype** — 4 completed screens.
2. **The Gemini Notebook source ledger** — proving the research basis.
3. **The AI Studio / Gemini prompt history link** — proving prompt architecture and critical curation.
