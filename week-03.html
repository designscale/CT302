Week 03 · Thursday, September 17 · Phase 1 — Research & Proposal

Live Site Audit

Auditing methodology; hands-on audit of the selected brand's homepage and PDP, desktop and mobile.

An audit is evidence, not opinion. Section by section, you name what's there, what's missing, and what it costs the user — with a screenshot attached to every claim.

Due this week

Live site audit

  • Homepage and PDP, desktop and mobile
  • Annotated screenshots for every finding
  • Pain points written from the user's perspective
  • At least three accessibility issues named specifically

Session plan ≈170 min

30 minAudit methodology
How to break a page into sections and interrogate each one: purpose, content, hierarchy, interaction, accessibility.
25 minHeuristics that matter in e-commerce
Product findability, image quality and zoom, variant selection, price clarity, shipping and returns visibility, add-to-cart feedback, trust signals.
60 minHands-on audit — desktop
Homepage then PDP. Screenshot, annotate, log. Nothing gets written that isn't visible in a capture.
35 minHands-on audit — mobile
Same two pages on a phone. Note what breaks, what's buried, and what's simply been cut.
20 minFindings round-robin
Two findings each, out loud. Vague findings get sharpened by the room.

In-class activity: Section-by-section teardown

  1. Capture the homepage and PDP at both breakpoints. Full-page screenshots, not viewport crops.
  2. Slice each page into named sections (hero, category grid, gallery, buy box, specs, cross-sell, footer).
  3. For each section, log: what it does · what's missing · the pain point · the opportunity.
  4. Run a contrast check on body text, prices and buttons. WCAG failures are the easiest points on the rubric to earn and the easiest to leave on the table.

Working with AI this week

Use AI to structure and stress-test your audit — not to see the site for you. It cannot look at the page you're auditing unless you show it.

Prompts to start from

  • “Here's my section-by-section audit of a PDP. Which standard e-commerce PDP elements have I not mentioned at all?”
  • “Rewrite this pain point so it names the user cost, not just the visual flaw.”

What to verify

  • Never let AI describe a page it hasn't been shown. If a finding isn't in your screenshots, it isn't a finding.

Evidence to log

These feed the cognitive-process rubric. Capture them as you work.

Domain Knowledge ApplicationAnnotated audit with contrast checks and named anti-patterns
Critical CurationWhere AI's suggested findings didn't survive contact with the actual site
