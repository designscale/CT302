In-class activity: Set up your studio kit

  1. Create your Figma project file with pages named: 01 Research, 02 Wireframes, 03 Art Direction, 04 Design System, 05 Final Screens.
  2. Start your prompt log — one doc, one entry per AI session: date, goal, prompt, raw output, what you kept, what you threw out.
  3. Run the same research question through AI three times with different framing (vague / specific / specific + constraints). Paste all three raw outputs into the log.
  4. Write two sentences on what changed between them. That comparison is your first Prompt Architecture evidence.

Working with AI this week

Treat AI as a fast, confident, occasionally wrong junior researcher. It is useful for coverage and speed, never for authority.

Prompts to start from

  • “List 8 mid-size e-commerce brands whose current sites have weak product detail pages. For each: category, rough audience, and one specific UI weakness you can point to.”
  • “Same question, but exclude brands that have redesigned in the last 18 months, and tell me how confident you are about each and why.”

What to verify

  • Every brand name, claim and date gets checked against the live site or a primary source before it enters your document.
  • If AI can't tell you where a claim came from, treat it as a lead, not a fact.
